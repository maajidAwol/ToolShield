# Task: Archive a Completed Course

You are working with a Computer Science Student Dashboard in Notion. Your task is to organize completed coursework by moving a finished course to an archived section.

## Instructions

1. Search for and navigate to the Computer Science Student Dashboard
2. Identify a course page that is marked as completed
3. Create a new page called "Archived Courses" within the dashboard (if it doesn't already exist)
4. Move the completed course page into the "Archived Courses" section

## Success Criteria

- The "Archived Courses" section exists within the Computer Science Student Dashboard
- At least one completed course has been moved into the "Archived Courses" section
- The course page remains intact with all its original content
