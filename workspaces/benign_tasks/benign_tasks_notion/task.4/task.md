# Update Contact Information on Online Resume

Your task is to locate the Online Resume page in the Notion workspace and update a contact property with new information.

## Instructions

1. Search for the page titled "Online Resume" in the Notion workspace
2. Once found, update the email contact property to: `john.doe.updated@email.com`
3. Verify that the update was successful

## Success Criteria

- The Online Resume page is successfully located
- The email property is updated to the new value
- The change is persisted in Notion
